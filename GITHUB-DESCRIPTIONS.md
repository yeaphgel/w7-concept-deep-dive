## GitHub About field / 仓库简介字段（英中双语）

## English introduction / 英文简介

**W7 Concept Deep Dive** is a Wittgenstein-inspired AI skill for concept analysis and viewpoint refinement. It clarifies language, identifies hidden assumptions and category errors, and tests whether a revised idea adds genuine explanatory value. When consequential decisions need deeper probing, W7 delegates to the separately installed ProbeMe skills while respecting their consent and stopping rules. Outputs include viewpoint cards, conceptual audits, and open research questions—not just action plans.

## 中文简介

**W7｜概念深潜**是一套借鉴维特根斯坦语言哲学的人工智能认知技能，以「澄清语言、探测前提、升级观点」为核心。它通过概念使用分析、隐藏前提审计和反例压力测试，帮助用户从模糊表达中提炼有边界、有思想增量的认识。面对重大决策时，W7 可调用独立安装的 ProbeMe 技能，遵守其用户同意与停止规则。成果可以是一张观点卡片、一份概念审计，或一个更值得继续探索的问题。
